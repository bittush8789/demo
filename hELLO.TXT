3.What is FNOL?

Answer:

FNOL stands for First Notice of Loss. It is the first notification received by the insurance company when a customer reports a loss or incident. The purpose is to capture and validate claim information so the claim can be processed further.


4. What types of claims did you handle?

5. How do you identify whether a claim belongs to Auto, Property, or Liability?
