# 😎 "Bad Boy" Psychology Roadmap (Healthy Version)

> In psychology, the traits often associated with a "bad boy" that people find attractive are usually **confidence, independence, assertiveness, and unpredictability** — not disrespect, manipulation, or harmful behavior.
